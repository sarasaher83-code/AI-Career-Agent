# Setting Up Job-Alert Emails

The system finds vacancies mainly through the job-alert emails that job sites send to your Gmail. You create the
alerts yourself, in your own accounts, once. The system never logs in to these sites.

**Use these search terms** (from the approved career positioning). Create one alert per line on each site where possible:

| Alert search terms | Locations |
|---|---|
| Design Manager | Saudi Arabia · United Arab Emirates |
| Head of Design / Design Management | Saudi Arabia · United Arab Emirates |
| Senior Design Manager | Saudi Arabia · United Arab Emirates · Qatar |
| Technical Office Manager / Head of Technical Office | Saudi Arabia · United Arab Emirates |
| Design Director | Saudi Arabia · United Arab Emirates |
| Development Manager (real estate) | Saudi Arabia · United Arab Emirates |
| Interior Design Manager | Saudi Arabia · United Arab Emirates |
| Owner's Representative | Saudi Arabia |
| مدير إدارة التصميم · مدير المكتب الفني | Saudi Arabia (Arabic-language sites and adverts) |

Choose **daily** emails where the site offers it.

---

## LinkedIn
1. Open **LinkedIn → Jobs**, type a search term (e.g. *Design Manager*) and a location (e.g. *Saudi Arabia*).
2. Optional filters: **Experience level → Director / Executive / Mid-Senior**.
3. Switch on **Set alert** (top of the results), then choose **Daily** and **Email**.
4. Repeat for each line of the table.

## Bayt
1. Search for a job title and country on bayt.com.
2. Click **Create job alert** (or the bell icon) on the results page and confirm daily emails.

## GulfTalent
1. In your GulfTalent account, open **Job alerts** (or search, then **Create alert**).
2. Add the titles and countries above.

## Naukrigulf (already sending you daily emails)
Your current daily "recommended jobs" are mostly general **Project Manager** roles, because they are based on your
profile. To improve them:
1. In your Naukrigulf profile, set the **desired job title** to *Design Manager / Head of Design Management / Technical Office Manager*.
2. Set **preferred locations** to Saudi Arabia and the UAE.
3. Under **Job alerts**, create alerts with the terms above.

## Indeed
1. Search on **sa.indeed.com** (Saudi Arabia) or **ae.indeed.com** (UAE).
2. Click **Get new jobs for this search by email**.

---

## What happens next
- Alerts arrive in your Gmail as usual. You don't need to create a label or move them.
- In the dashboard, **Job Discovery → Import from Gmail** reads them (read-only) and adds the vacancies, with duplicates merged.
- **Naukrigulf** emails are fully supported now. **LinkedIn, Bayt, GulfTalent and Indeed** alert formats are added as
  soon as their first alert emails arrive: each reader is built and tested against a real email before it is switched on.
- Until Gmail access is set up on your computer, you can upload a single alert email in the dashboard
  (in Gmail: open the email → ⋮ → **Download message**, then **Upload an alert email**).
