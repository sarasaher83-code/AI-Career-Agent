# Career Desk: User Guide

Your Career Desk is a private page on claude.ai. Only you can open it or read its data.
Open it from the link in `cloud/DESK_URL`, or in claude.ai under **Artifacts**.

## Your week at a glance

| When | What happens | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Daily, about 9 AM Cairo | **Job Scout** finds new jobs, scores them and puts the top 5 on **Today**. It builds a CV and cover letter for every job you approved the day before | Approve or skip the new jobs (2 minutes) |
| When you approve an Easy Apply job | Nothing automatic | Tap **Copy apply instructions**, paste them into the Claude desktop app and let it apply (max 5 a day) |
| When you approve any other job | Its pack is built at the next 9 AM run | Download the CV and cover letter, apply on the job page, tap **Mark applied** |
| Sunday / Tuesday / Thursday, 8:30 AM Riyadh | Your approved post is due | Content tab: **Copy posting instructions** → Claude desktop app, or copy the post and paste it into LinkedIn yourself; then **Mark posted** |
| Before Monday 7 PM | | Growth tab: enter this week's LinkedIn numbers (from your profile's Analytics) |
| Monday, about 7 PM Cairo | **Growth Report** compares the numbers, reviews the pipeline, gives 3 actions and drafts next week's 3 posts | Read it; approve the new posts |
| Any time | | Recruiters tab: update a firm's status after you contact it |

## The tabs

- **Today:** jobs waiting for your decision, approved jobs, and apply instructions.
  Each job shows its match %, the 8-part score (hover a bar for its evidence), reasons, gaps, and anything the advert does not state.
- **Pipeline:** every job, filterable by status and country.
- **Content:** your LinkedIn posts: approve, copy, post, mark posted.
- **Engagement:** daily comment drafts and people worth following. You post, comment and connect yourself.
- **Recruiters:** 11 firms and employers, with how to engage each, your status and notes, and a fact-checked intro note.
- **Growth:** weekly numbers form, weekly report, and your LinkedIn profile makeover (each section has a Copy button).
- **Analytics:** jobs found, approved and applied; average match; posts published; recruiters engaged; LinkedIn numbers week on week.
- **Activity:** a log of every scheduled run.

## Rules the system always follows
- Everything it writes about you comes from your locked master profile, and every text passes the fact checker.
  If something about you changes (a new certificate, a new project you may disclose), tell Claude so the profile is updated first.
- No financial figures from MENA, ever. Nojoud Lodge is shown as pipeline.
- Nothing is applied to or posted unless you tapped **Approve** for that item.
- It never comments, likes, messages, follows or connects on LinkedIn.

## If something looks wrong
- **No new jobs in the morning:** open **Activity**. The run's line says what it read and what failed.
- **"Gmail unavailable" in Activity:** add Gmail to the Job Scout routine (claude.ai → Routines → Job Scout → Edit → Connectors).
- **A job's facts look wrong:** tap **Skip** and tell Claude. Scores from search snippets are flagged and less reliable.
- **You want to change a schedule or pause a routine:** claude.ai → Routines. "Sara Daily Job Search" (the older routine) is paused.
